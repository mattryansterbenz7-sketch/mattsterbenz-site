Design notes (scratch only, won't ship)

What changed vs current home:

  • Hero is now split-screen. Resume positioning lives on the left; a live Coop product card lives on the right. The case study isn't tucked into "Tools & Projects" anymore — it's the hero proof point.
  • Headline pivot: "I close revenue. And I build the systems behind it." — promotes the builder identity from sub-claim to co-headline. The Coop card immediately backs it up so it doesn't read as marketing fluff.
  • Coop card uses the integrations graph, recycled from /coop/integrations.html. Same visual signature, same warm/dark palette as the rest of the /coop/ subsite — so when readers click through, the design language is consistent.
  • The "proof band" below the hero is a single sentence that explicitly connects "operator who ships" → "Coop is what that looks like." Closes the gap between resume and case study.
  • Three-up cards replace the long Tools & Projects section with a cleaner "what I do / what I build / what I want" trio. Each card links somewhere meaningful (case studies, Coop, contact).

What's preserved: Same visual tokens (Barlow + Inter, cream surface, rust accent #c8401a). Same nav structure with Coop as a tab. Same end goals (Get in Touch CTA, case studies link).

What's NOT in this mockup yet: highlights, principles, testimonials, looking-for grid, contact form, footer. Those existing sections would still live below the three-up.

If you like this direction, the lift to ship it: mostly an in-place hero rewrite of mattsterbenz.com/index.html. The Coop card SVG can be reused as-is. A few hours of work, not days.
