What's happening

Every saved opportunity that hasn't been triaged lands in the queue, pre-scored against your profile. Pass moves it to Rejected. Apply opens the posting in a new tab, auto-binds Coop in the side panel, and shifts the stage to Applied.

01.Pre-scored on saveThe score, top three flags, and a one-line verdict are already on the card by the time you see it.
02.Two-tap triageDrag, swipe, click, or use ← / → arrow keys. Built for fast triage when 30 postings just dropped.
03.The pipeline updates itselfApply shifts the entry to the right stage with a timestamp. No spreadsheet bookkeeping.
