Speed to lead is a math problem

Seven minute read · Demand generation

Every inbound funnel has the same leak, and almost nobody measures it. A visitor arrives with intent, spends four minutes reading, fills out a form, and then waits. The wait is where the deal dies.

The window is smaller than your process

Buying intent is perishable. Someone comparing three vendors on a Tuesday afternoon is not in the same headspace on Thursday morning. By the time a rep works the queue, the visitor has read two competitor pages and cooled off.

The question isn't whether you follow up. It's whether you follow up while they still care.

Coverage beats effort

Most teams try to solve this by pushing reps to respond faster. That works until someone takes a vacation, or traffic spikes after a campaign, or a visitor lands at 11pm from a time zone nobody staffs.

The durable fix isn't a faster human. It's a conversation that starts the moment intent appears, and a human who joins when the conversation is worth their time.

What to measure instead

Stop tracking form fills. Track conversations started, conversations that reached qualification, and meetings booked without a human touching the thread. Those three numbers tell you whether your site is a brochure or a pipeline source.
